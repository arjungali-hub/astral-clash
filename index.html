How to Play

🏆

Objective

Best of 3 rounds. Win a round by knocking your opponent's HP to 0 — there's no clock to just run out, so see Arena Collapse below for how a round is guaranteed to end.

👁️

First-Person View

You fight through your character's own eyes, weapon in hand. Turn keys rotate your view like looking around, not an instant sidestep — walk forward/back in whatever direction you're currently facing to actually move that way. Two human players share the screen split left/right; against a bot you get the whole screen.

🧱

Arena Collapse

No round timer — instead, a countdown to the next collapse. The whole arena closes in from all four sides on a timer (the edges literally crumble away), squeezing the fight toward the center so stalling isn't an option. If it fully shuts, both fighters are crushed and lose the same flat HP per second (regardless of max or current HP) until one dies — whoever's crushed first loses.

🏃

Movement & Jumping

Turn to look around, then walk forward or back in that direction — there's no strafing sideways. Jumping reaches platforms and cover, but has a cooldown afterward — you can't spam it. Steps with a small enough rise are walkable without jumping; tall ledges and walls aren't. Use columns and interior walls for cover.

⚔️

Basic vs. Special

Basic attacks are fast and always available (short cooldown between swings) and hit hard, so don't get comfortable eating them. Your Special needs a full meter — meter builds from both dealing and taking damage, so a losing fighter charges up faster. Each fighter's Special is unique: some blink and combo, some launch, stun, root, burn, pull the enemy in, or strike from range. Read yours (and your opponent's) in the fighter panel.

⚔️

The Roster

Ten fighters, each with a distinct weapon and range: sword, twin daggers, gauntlets, warhammer, scythe, vine-whip and more, plus ranged casters who fling shards, orbs and lightning bolts. Reach and speed vary a lot — read a fighter's stats before you commit to them.

💨

Dash

A short burst with brief invulnerability frames — phases through attacks and projectiles entirely if timed right. Has its own cooldown, so it's a precise tool, not a shield.

🩹

No Healing

There's no way to get HP back — no health pickups, no regen. Every hit you take counts. Play patient, punish mistakes, and use your dash i-frames and shields to avoid damage rather than trying to out-heal it.
