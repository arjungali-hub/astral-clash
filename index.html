How to Play

🏆

Objective

Best of 3 rounds. Win a round by knocking your opponent's HP to 0 — there's no clock to just run out, so see Arena Collapse below for how a round is guaranteed to end.

🧱

Arena Collapse

No round timer — instead, a countdown to the next wall shrink. The play area's side walls pull inward on a timer, squeezing the fight into a shrinking strip so stalling isn't an option. If the walls fully close, both fighters take the same flat damage per second (regardless of max or current HP) until one dies — whoever's crushed first loses.

🏃

Movement & Jumping

Move freely in all directions. Jumping clears pillars and reaches platforms, but has a cooldown afterward — you can't spam it. Steps with a small enough rise are walkable without jumping; tall ledges aren't.

⚔️

Basic vs. Special

Both hit hard — a handful of clean attacks can end a round, so don't get comfortable eating hits. Basic attacks are fast and always available (short cooldown between swings). Specials hit even harder but need a full meter — meter builds from both dealing and taking damage, so a losing fighter charges up faster.

⚔️

The Roster

Ten fighters, each with a distinct weapon and range: sword, twin daggers, gauntlets, warhammer, scythe, vine-whip and more, plus ranged casters who fling shards, orbs and lightning bolts. Reach and speed vary a lot — read a fighter's stats before you commit to them.

💨

Dodge

A short dash with brief invulnerability frames — phases through attacks and projectiles entirely if timed right. Has its own cooldown, so it's a precise tool, not a shield.

⚠️

Hazards & Pickups

A red zone telegraphs where a damage hazard is about to activate — get out before it does. A glowing orb periodically appears on the map and heals whoever reaches it first.

🎮

Above All Else

Have fun playing! Pick whoever looks fun, mash the buttons a bit, and see what sticks.
